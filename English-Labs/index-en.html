Interactive Virtual Labs for Chemical, Biological & Pharmaceutical Engineering Teaching

This collection brings together nine free interactive virtual labs for chemical, biological and pharmaceutical engineering teaching. Each lab lets students change operating parameters and watch the system respond in real time: draw the stages on a distillation diagram, compare two ideal reactors, tune a fermenter, or scale a bioreactor from bench to pilot scale. The labs are built around the equations taught in class, so every curve on screen can be traced back to a model students already know. All labs are interactive demonstrations for teaching.Demo

For teachers: each lab page includes a short concept introduction and frequently asked questions, and can be used as a pre-lab exercise, an in-class demonstration, or a self-study assignment.

Chemical Engineering

  • interactive McCabe-Thiele diagram — Distillation lab: step off theoretical stages for a benzene-toluene column and locate the feed stage.
  • shell and tube heat exchanger simulation — Heat transfer lab: vary cold-water flow and steam temperature, then read outlet temperature and LMTD.
  • CSTR vs PFR conversion comparison — Reactor lab: compare conversion in a stirred tank and a plug flow reactor for a first-order reaction.
  • Darcy friction factor vs Reynolds number — Fluid flow lab: read pressure drop and plot friction factor against Reynolds number in laminar and turbulent flow.

Biological Engineering

  • Monod kinetics microbial growth simulation — Batch culture lab: adjust μmax and initial substrate, track OD600 through lag, log, stationary and death phases.
  • kLa oxygen transfer coefficient simulation — Fermentation lab: set aeration and agitation, balance oxygen supply against uptake, and hold dissolved oxygen above the critical level.
  • Michaelis-Menten kinetics enzyme lab — Enzyme kinetics lab: vary substrate concentration and read Km and Vmax from a Lineweaver-Burk plot.
  • bioreactor scale-up simulation — Scale-up lab: scale a 1 L vessel to 100 L and compare impeller speeds under constant P/V, tip speed and Reynolds number.

Pharmaceutical Engineering

  • penicillin fermentation simulation — Antibiotic fermentation lab: run a fed-batch penicillin fermentation and watch biomass, dissolved oxygen and product build over time.
